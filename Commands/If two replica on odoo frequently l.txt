If two replica on odoo frequently logout on browser refresh :
Solution:
Service — added:
  sessionAffinity: ClientIP
  sessionAffinityConfig:
    clientIP:
      timeoutSeconds: 10800   # 3 hours

odoo-nodeport Service — added the same:
  sessionAffinity: ClientIP
  sessionAffinityConfig:
    clientIP:
      timeoutSeconds: 10800   # 3 hours


kubectl apply -f odoo.yaml -n nidan

Verify the affinity took effect:
kubectl get svc odoo -n nidan -o jsonpath='{.spec.sessionAffinity}{"\n"}'
kubectl get svc odoo-nodeport -n nidan -o jsonpath='{.spec.sessionAffinity}{"\n"}'

Quick sanity check after applying:
# Confirm pods are still healthy
kubectl get pods -n nidan -l app=odoo

# Confirm services now have sessionAffinity
kubectl get svc odoo odoo-nodeport -n nidan -o custom-columns=\
NAME:.metadata.name,AFFINITY:.spec.sessionAffinity,PORT:.spec.ports[0].port

If both show ClientIP, you're done. Test in the browser — refresh several times and you should stay logged in.

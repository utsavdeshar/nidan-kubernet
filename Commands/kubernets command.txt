Services: ( To make access available externally to user and internal networking between pods services in kub.)
   -NodePort: The application is exposed to particular port and exposed externally (30001 port) the redirect request to target port 80
   - targetport: the port in which application pods are is listen on and not exposed externally (80 port)
   -service port 80 : Used by internal communication by pods with in the nodes
